HOW TO PLAY

THE RULES

SPINThe light hops the 18 squares. Stop it and you take whatever face is showing.
WHAMMYWipes your bank to zero. Four of them and you're eliminated.
PASSOnly your own earned spins can be passed, and only to the current leader.
+1 SPINSome faces hand you another spin. Doubles, Add-a-One and Pick a Corner are on the board too.
MOVEMove One Space shifts the light one square and pays out free.
OVERCLOCKSometimes offered at the start of a turn: cash ×2 for 3 spins, but an extra Whammy sits on the board.
ENDLESSSpend your bank on power-ups between levels. Every 5th level is a boss: the light reverses once each spin.
