YOUR OWN LITTLE STATION

Program a channel.

Paste Internet Archive item links, one per line. We’ll find the playable MP4s and turn them into a continuous channel.

Use videos you have permission to stream. Your custom channels stay in this browser; export a backup to move them elsewhere.

A little like cable. A lot less cable.

These are scheduled Internet Archive videos, presented as television. Everyone watching the same channel joins the same point in its looping schedule, based on their device clock.

Channels keep rolling when you leave. Tap “Manage channels” to add Archive item links. Custom channels are saved on this device, with export and import for backup.

Source: 1VA’s Internet Archive uploads. Only original-series episode items are included by default. Season and episode labels follow the uploader and may conflict with filenames; episode identity and order have not been independently verified.

Availability and buffering depend on Internet Archive. A video being hosted there does not establish permission to rebroadcast it.

Keyboard: ↑ / ↓ change channel · M toggles sound · F opens full screen.
