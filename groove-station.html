GROOVE STATION

Six styles of house, start to finish in your browser. Every sound is generated live — there are no audio files to load. Tap a pad, get a loop; tap four, get a groove.

SOUND

How it works

Pads A B C D
Each row is an instrument, each pad is a one-bar loop. Tap to bring it in, tap the lit pad to drop it out.
Instant / Bar sync
Instant is the default: a pad takes effect the moment you press it, and anything already playing on that row is cut dead. Switch to Bar sync if you'd rather changes wait for the next barline so nothing can land off-grid.
Performance FX
One row, all hold-to-use, all instant — nothing waits for a beat or a bar. Hold for as long as you want the effect, let go and you're straight back.
Echo tempo-synced dotted-eighth repeats with the groove ducked underneath, so it works for a drop as well as an exit. The tail rings out when you release.
Reverb opens a long hall. Release and the tail decays on its own.
Flanger and Phaser add sweeping movement — flanger is the jet whoosh, phaser the rotating-speaker swirl. Both run continuously under the hood, so the button is only a fader.
Roll grabs the last sixteenth and repeats it. The dry signal stays up just long enough to fill the line, so there's no gap going in and none coming out.
Bass cut ducks the kick and bass about 24 dB rather than muting them.
DJ filter
The knob below the row. Centre is off; turn left to sweep the low-pass down, right to sweep the high-pass up. Resonance comes in on its own as you leave centre.
Remix
Swaps the keys or the bass for another style's engine — soulful piano over a dub tech groove, organ bass under deep house. Press it until something surprises you. X remixes, C puts the stock sounds back.
Fill
Drops a one-bar snare and tom roll, then returns to the pattern.
Rec
Captures live audio to a .webm file you can drop straight into a DAW.
Audio: smooth or tight
Smooth is the default and uses a larger output buffer, which is what stops crackling — especially on Windows and on Bluetooth earbuds. Tight uses the smallest buffer the browser allows for the fastest possible response, at the risk of crackle. Reload the page after changing it.
Your work is kept
Every pattern, chance value, step count, level and mute is saved as you go, per kit, and comes back when you reopen the page. Switching kits and back keeps your edits too. The button at the bottom restores the factory patterns for the kit you're on — it asks once before it does.
